How AegisWatch works

01

Sponsor funds a Program

A project escrows a bounty pool and locks a per-tier payout schedule (HIGH ≥ MEDIUM ≥ LOW) plus a minimum reporter bond. The scope URL and summary are recorded on-chain.

create_program(...)
02

Researcher files a Report

Anyone can submit a report against an open program: a summary, evidence URLs (advisory, PR, on-chain trace, mirrored post), and a bond ≥ the program's minimum. Bond is anti-spam collateral.

submit_report(...)
03

AI Jury reaches consensus

convene_verifier fires the on-chain nondet block. Each validator independently fetches every evidence URL, runs a security-triage prompt, and votes. Consensus requires severity within one tier and injection flags in agreement.

convene_verifier(...) · nondet
04

Contract settles the bounty

HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW: reporter withdraws payout + bond. INSUFFICIENT_PROOF: bond returned, no payout. DEBUNKED: bond forfeited to the pool. The contract re-verifies the arithmetic post-consensus.

withdraw_report(...)
